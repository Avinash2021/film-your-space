How I work

Four steps, every time

  1. 01

    Understand

    Sit with stakeholders, read the requirements nobody wants to read, and find the real question hiding under the brief.

  2. 02

    Research

    Talk to the people who'll use it. Map journeys, audit competitors, and separate what users say from what they do.

  3. 03

    Design

    Flows, wireframes, then high-fidelity UI on a system that scales — components and patterns, not one-off screens.

  4. 04

    Ship & refine

    Pair with engineering through build, watch what breaks in the real world, and iterate on evidence rather than taste.

About me

A designer who reads the
requirements doc.

My practice sits between research and craft — reading a messy set of requirements, finding the shape of the actual problem, and building an interface that carries it without friction. I work in Figma, think in flows, and I'm comfortable being the first (and sometimes only) designer in the room.

Before design I studied international finance, which turns out to be very useful when the product is a bank. Off the clock I train in Kathak — a classical Indian dance built entirely on counted rhythm — write poetry, play ukulele, and photograph whatever plant life I can find nearby.
